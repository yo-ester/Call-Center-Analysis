# Call Center Optimization: Reducing Abandon Rate and Improving Customer Satisfaction

## Business Problem
The call center shows a high abandon rate (20%) and low customer satisfaction (2.98/5), indicating inefficiencies in handling incoming calls and potential customer loss.

## Key Insights
- High abandon rate suggests long waiting times
- Peak hours are not properly staffed
- Performance variability across agents impacts service quality
- Low CSAT indicates inefficient call resolution or poor experience

## Recommendations
- Shift optimization: increase agents during peak hours
- Agent training: improve FCR → fewer callbacks
- Call routing: assign complex calls to the best agents
- Reduce wait time: callback system or queue management

## Estimated impact
- Reduce abandon rate from 20% → 12%
- Increase CSAT from 2.98 → 3.5
- Improve FCR from 70% → 78%
